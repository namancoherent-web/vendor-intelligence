Market: Global Remote Elderly Health Monitoring Market
Geography: Global

What are the different entities in the remote elderly health monitoring value chain based on
functionality, and which should be considered for market sizing? This market covers technology
used to monitor seniors' health, safety, and daily activity outside traditional clinical settings
— spanning RPM platforms, connected sensors, and managed remote-care services aimed at aging
populations.

FUNCTIONAL ENTITIES IN THE VALUE CHAIN (these are the segments to INCLUDE and profile):

1. Device-Agnostic Platform, Application & Dashboard Providers
   - Function: Provide the software layer that ingests data from multiple device types and
     delivers clinical dashboards, care-team workflows, patient apps, alerts, and analytics for
     remote elderly monitoring — without being tied to a single hardware OEM.
   - Core entities: RPM / virtual-care platforms, device-agnostic data hubs, and care-coordination
     software vendors (e.g. Biofourmis, Huma, Validic, Health Recovery Solutions, Life365 Health,
     Luscii, Sensi.AI, VitalTech, Current Health, CareSimple, VivaSmartCare).
   - Business model: SaaS platform fees, per-patient-per-month (PPPM) subscriptions, enterprise
     licensing to health systems / payers, and value-based care enablement.
   - Market sizing: INCLUDE — platform and application revenue is a core spend bucket; count
     recurring software / monitoring-service fees paid for elderly remote-care programs.

2. Remote Monitoring Device, Sensor & Managed Care Providers
   - Function: Supply the physical monitoring layer and/or managed remote-care service for
     seniors — wearables, ambient sensors, connected hubs, fall / activity detection, vital-sign
     peripherals, and 24/7 response or care-coordination services bundled with hardware.
   - Core entities: Elderly-focused sensor and wearable makers, PERS / ambient-monitoring vendors,
     and managed remote-care providers (e.g. CarePredict, Essence SmartCare, Withings, Tenovi,
     Tunstall Group, UnaliWear, Vayyar Care, Medsien, Prevounce).
   - Business model: Device sale or lease + recurring monitoring subscription; B2B2C through
     payers, home-care agencies, and senior-living operators.
   - Market sizing: INCLUDE — hardware and bundled managed-care monitoring revenue where the
     end product is remote elderly health / safety monitoring (not general consumer fitness).

Which entities to include in a market sizing activity?

Top-tier research firms typically size the remote patient monitoring / elderly monitoring market
across two primary buckets: Platforms & Software and Devices & Managed Monitoring Services.

  INCLUDE: Device-Agnostic Platform, Application & Dashboard Providers
  Count recurring platform, application, and RPM software revenue where the use case is elderly
  remote health monitoring. These vendors monetize the care-coordination and data layer.

  INCLUDE: Remote Monitoring Device, Sensor & Managed Care Providers
  Count hardware and bundled managed-monitoring service revenue sold for senior remote care —
  wearables, ambient sensors, hubs, and monitoring subscriptions tied to elderly health programs.

  EXCLUDE: General acute-care EHR / hospital IT vendors
  Hospital information systems and generic EHR vendors are adjacent infrastructure, not the
  remote elderly monitoring product market.

  EXCLUDE: Pure home-care agencies and payers (unless they sell proprietary monitoring tech)
  Revenue from delivering in-home nursing or insurance claims is the care-delivery / payer
  market, not the monitoring technology market — unless the entity also sells its own platform
  or device (then include only that product revenue).

  EXCLUDE / CONSOLIDATE: General consumer fitness wearables with no elderly-care positioning
  Do not include pure fitness-tracker revenue; only count vendors whose products and GTM are
  positioned for senior health, RPM, aging-in-place, or managed elderly monitoring.

  EXCLUDE: Pharmaceutical and clinical-trial-only remote monitoring
  Trial-enablement or drug-adherence tools outside elderly care programs belong in adjacent
  digital-health segments, not this landscape.

Include for the company landscape: both segments above.
Sizing principle: capture end-user spend on elderly remote-monitoring technology — platform
software and device / managed-care bundles — without double-counting payer reimbursement or
generic hospital IT that does not serve this use case.
