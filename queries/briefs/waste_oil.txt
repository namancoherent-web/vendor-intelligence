Market: Waste Oil Market
Geography: Global

What are the different entities in the Waste Oil Market value chain based on functionality,
and which should be considered for market sizing? This market covers the collection,
aggregation, processing, recycling, and re-refining of used/waste oil into recovered base oil
and recycled fuel products.

FUNCTIONAL ENTITIES IN THE VALUE CHAIN (these are the segments to INCLUDE and profile):

1. Lubricant Manufacturers / Waste Oil Generators
   - Function: produce lubricants and generate used/waste oil through automotive, industrial,
     marine, construction, mining, and machinery operations.
   - Core entities: lubricant manufacturers, automotive service centers, fleet operators,
     garages, industrial plants, manufacturing facilities, power plants, marine operators.
   - Market sizing: partial — use for waste-oil feedstock VOLUME; exclude their virgin
     lubricant sales revenue to avoid overstatement.

2. Used Oil Collection / Aggregation Companies
   - Function: collect, transport, store, test, and aggregate used oil from many generation
     points before recycling/re-refining/fuel processing.
   - Core entities: licensed used-oil collectors, waste management companies, oil recovery
     service providers, collection depots, aggregators, logistics providers.
   - Market sizing: INCLUDE (first monetized layer) — collection/handling fees + resale.

3. Waste Oil Processing / Re-Refining Companies
   - Function: treat used oil via dehydration, filtration, distillation, vacuum distillation,
     hydrotreating, solvent extraction, and clay treatment to recover base oil, fuel oil, and
     by-products.
   - Core entities: waste-oil recyclers, re-refiners, used-oil treatment plants, base-oil
     recovery companies, industrial oil recycling operators.
   - Market sizing: PRIMARY CORE SEGMENT — treatment/gate fees + re-refined base oil sales +
     recycled fuel oil sales.

4. Recovered Oil / Base Oil Utilization / Downstream Offtake Companies
   - Function: purchase and use re-refined base oil, recycled fuel oil, and recovered oil in
     lubricant blending, industrial heating, marine fuels, and refinery feedstock.
   - Core entities: lubricant blenders, oil marketers, industrial fuel users, refineries,
     petrochemical companies, marine fuel suppliers.
   - Market sizing: include only the PURCHASE VALUE of recovered/re-refined products, not the
     full company revenue.

Include for the company landscape: all four segments above.
Treated for sizing only (do NOT double-count): count the final monetized processed-oil value
(re-refined base oil / recycled fuel oil / collection-treatment service revenue).
Out of scope (exclude): virgin lubricant manufacturing revenue; recycling equipment / plant /
EPC technology providers; regulatory, compliance and environmental testing consultants.
